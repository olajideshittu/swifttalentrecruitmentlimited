Description

Restore the elasticity of your skin with a bundle of natural ingredients ranging from Mediterranean algae, Patina Panzica, among others. Elemis Pro-Collagen Marine Cream will dramatically increase the quantity of glycosaminoglycan within the skin. These water-attracting cells will revive the suppleness, firmness and elasticity of your skin and return hydration to it. In addition, a blended combination of Rose and Mimosa absolutes create the ultimate anti-aging protection.
